🌿 Nature & Ecology

  • Bird keeping - I share my home with a few lively feathered friends. Observing their behavior and learning their habits is one of the most calming parts of my day. They've taught me patience, attentiveness, and how to appreciate the unique personality of each living being.
  • Turtle keeping - Turtles move slowly, and their pace has reminded me that not everything in life needs to be fast. Caring for them has been a lesson in the philosophy of slowness—of patience, quiet observation, and long-term companionship.
  • Fruit tree growing - I cultivate guava and grapes through the dance of sunlight, rain, and seasons. I am not merely a grower—I am an observer of data. By monitoring weather patterns, temperature, humidity, and UV intensity, I fine-tune each cultivation decision with precision. Leftover bird feed, fallen feathers, and natural droppings are carefully fermented and transformed into nourishment for the soil—a dialogue between technology and nature, a practice of regenerative ecology. Through data comparison and outcome validation, I've witnessed the correlation between environmental metrics and fruit quality, confirming the tangible impact of ecological cycles on soil health. From seed to fruit, this journey transcends simple growth; it is a poetic fusion of environmental stewardship and data-driven agriculture.

I enjoy turning abstract ideas of daily life into tangible, livable space. Design isn't just about aesthetics; it's also about flow, function, storage, and user experience—principles that overlap with product design.
